Quiet calibration measures and saves the noise floor with motors silent. Full intensity is reserved for envelope readings near 970 / 1023. Optional: place all microphones side by side, play one steady sound equally to them, then Match microphones. No vibration at the measured noise floor. Above it, sensitivity lifts quieter activity and contrast reduces the quieter sides. Motor frequencies: left/red LF 95 Hz · back/white HF 160 Hz · right/yellow MF 130 Hz. Save settings to retain adjustments after restart.

TITAN setup & motor trims

Leave the TITAN mode jumper removed. Its RXD/TXD header shares the USB UART: use one transmitting source at a time. Identify the installed VH version. Each individual qualification test is one second at full command. The one-second unequal overlap uses left 40%, back 70%, right 100% to keep the weaker motors perceptible. Confirm the named motors start, remain independent, and stop. Stock finite effects need a smooth-playback check before normal use.
